# CzechCraft

> Czech-themed food, drinks and items for Minecraft.

CzechCraft is a small, friendly Fabric mod that adds traditional Czech food and drinks to Minecraft. Affectionate, not slick — small, lovingly-rendered items that fit naturally into vanilla survival.

---

## What's in v1

### Rohlík — the Czech bread roll

A traditional crescent-shaped bread roll, dusted with poppy seeds.

- **Crafted from 2 wheat** placed horizontally adjacent.
  - Works in a 3×3 crafting table.
  - Also works in your 2×2 inventory grid — handy for early-game survival.
- **Restores 2 hunger drumsticks** (nutrition 4, saturation modifier 0.3) — between vanilla cookie and bread.
- **Stack size 64** — easy to bring on long expeditions.
- Custom illustration-style 16×16 texture.

## Installation

1. Install the [Fabric loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/) for Minecraft 26.1.
2. Install [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api).
3. Download the latest `czechcraft-X.Y.Z.jar` from this Modrinth page.
4. Drop the jar into `.minecraft/mods/`.
5. Launch the game and look for **Rohlík** in the **CzechCraft** creative tab — or just place 2 wheat side by side in a crafting grid.
